1. System Overview & Key Business Benefits

OZONE Enterprise ERP is a multi-company, multi-user vapour-and-diaper distribution ERP. It runs as a Next.js web application at https://ozoneso.vercel.app and as a Windows desktop wrapper that opens the same web application. Data is stored per company in Supabase Postgres; access is controlled by custom 12-hour sessions, role permissions and row-level security.

The core document chain is strict: Sales Order → GDN / Gate Pass → Sales Tax Invoice → Bank Receipt → Customer Ledger & Reports. Each stage can only be created by the correct role, and a document cannot be edited once its downstream document exists.

Problems it solves

  • Paper vouchers, duplicate voucher numbers and manual register errors.
  • Finished-goods stock mismatches between orders, dispatch and invoices.
  • Manual calculation of 18% sales tax, 4% further tax and Section 236G advance tax.
  • No clear view of customer dues, aging, booking agents and bank accounts.
  • Staff seeing prices, other companies' data or documents outside their job role.
  • Owners unable to control demo periods, licences, users or company access.

Time-saving and accuracy benefits

  • Automatic yearly voucher sequences with atomic counters — no duplicated numbers.
  • Live FG stock: cartons and pieces update automatically only when a GDN is posted.
  • Sales order status recalculates automatically as PENDING, PARTIAL_DELIVERED or DELIVERED.
  • Auto tax engine for registered and unregistered customers, with amount-in-words.
  • Instant receivables aging buckets: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days.
  • Print-ready A4 vouchers with company logo, bank details and signatures.
  • Customer self-service portal for placing and tracking orders.
  • Company isolation, hidden-rate accounts, entry locks and licence locks enforced in the database.
Roles in this system: SUPER_ADMIN, COMPANY_ADMIN, STORE_FG, ACCOUNTS, CUSTOMER.

2. Getting Started & Access Guide

Open the application

  1. Web: open https://ozoneso.vercel.app in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Desktop: launch OZONE Enterprise ERP. It opens the same URL; an internet connection is required. If offline, a branded retry screen appears — check the connection and click Retry Connection.
  3. Enter the Company Code (default demo company: OZONE). The system verifies the company automatically and may show the company name and logo.
  4. Enter your Username and Password, then click Sign in.
  5. Sessions last 12 hours and are stored only in this browser. Always click Sign out when finished on a shared computer.

After login

  • CUSTOMER users land directly in the Customer Portal with only Place New Order and Track My Orders.
  • All other roles land on the Dashboard with a role-filtered sidebar.
  • Click Change Password in the header to update your own password (minimum 6 characters).
  • If your account has hidden rates, a banner confirms that rate and amount columns are intentionally blanked.

First-time organisation setup (Super Admin / Company Admin)

  1. Administration → create users for Store, Accounts and each customer contact.
  2. Products / FG Stock → add products with size, type, HS code, diapers per bag, bags per carton and opening cartons.
  3. Customers → add customer master records with CNIC/NTN, STRN, registration status, filer status, bank account and branch.
  4. Administration → set the company logo, bank details and licence information (Super Admin only for company profile).
  5. For a new company, Super Admin uses the company wizard, imports product/customer CSVs and downloads the build configuration.
Demo and licence: demo companies auto-lock 7 days after the demo start date. The login screen then shows the owner contact and a WhatsApp link. A locked/blocked company cannot be used until the owner extends the demo or converts it to a full licence.

3. Role-by-Role Step-by-Step Operations Manual

3.1 SUPER_ADMIN — Master Owner

Full access to every company and module. In addition to daily operations, the Super Admin performs governance in Administration → Super Admin — Company Master Control:

  1. Use Suspend / Activate to block or restore a company.
  2. Use +7 Days to extend a demo, or Convert Full to remove demo mode.
  3. Use Factory Reset only with written approval: type the exact company code, confirm, and the system clears the master ledger, restores opening stock and resets voucher sequences while preserving customers, products, companies and users.
  4. Use New Company Setup & Template Wizard to create a company, import CSV master data, set expiry and download the desktop build config.
  5. Company profile, logo, licence dates and owner contact are editable only by Super Admin.
  6. Confidential founder playbooks are available only through the owner-only in-app section and are never shown to other roles.

3.2 COMPANY_ADMIN — Company Manager

  1. Manage users: create/edit accounts, set roles, link customers to booker/customer logins, set permissions, hide rates or allow lock bypass.
  2. Create customer logins from the Customers page and copy the ready WhatsApp credential message.
  3. Use all voucher modules, products, customers, ledger, reports and printing.
  4. Change passwords for your own account; you cannot modify company profile/logo or create another SUPER_ADMIN.

3.3 STORE_FG — Store / Finished Goods

  1. Open Sales Orders and review orders with pending cartons.
  2. Open GDN & Gate Pass → New GDN / Gate Pass, select the sales order, enter delivered cartons, vehicle, driver and remarks.
  3. Click Post GDN & Gate Pass. Stock is deducted immediately and the sales order status updates automatically.
  4. Print the GDN/Gate Pass using the view/print action and hand it to the dispatch team.
  5. Do not delete a GDN after an invoice exists — the database blocks it.

3.4 ACCOUNTS — Accounts / Finance

  1. Open Sales Tax Invoices → New invoice, choose an unbilled GDN, verify rates and tax, then generate the invoice.
  2. Open Bank Receipts → New receipt, choose an unpaid invoice or enter the amount, bank account and reference, then post the receipt.
  3. Use Customer Ledger to review invoiced, received and balance amounts by customer/booker and print the ledger.
  4. Use Reports & Exports for aging, FG stock sheet, ledger CSV and full Excel export.

3.5 CUSTOMER / Distributor — Customer Portal

  1. Log in with the credentials shared by your company administrator.
  2. On Place New Order, select a linked customer (registered customers are queued first), enter cartons per product and use Save & Next.
  3. Review the auto-grouped order queue and click Submit All Customer Orders. Each customer becomes a separate sales order.
  4. On Track My Orders, refresh to see live status: Order Placed → Preparing / Partial Dispatch → Dispatched & On the Way → Invoiced & Completed.
  5. Print invoices where permitted. Rates and amounts are hidden for customer accounts by design.

4. Reports, Analytics, Security Rules & FAQs

Dashboard

  • KPIs: total sales orders, total revenue, fulfilment rate and recovery rate.
  • Monthly chart compares Sales Order Value and Invoiced Revenue in PKR.
  • Customer insights show repeat customers, active customers, average order value and registration segments.
  • System status cards show Web Portal, Customer App, FG Inventory, Bank Ledger and Supabase Sync; Offline means that data source failed to load.

Reports & Exports

  • Ledger CSV exports customer ledger rows.
  • Full Excel export exports the company workbook via XLSX.
  • Aging applies receipts FIFO to invoices and buckets outstanding amounts into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days.
  • Printable ledger/report titles are booker/agent-wise and include bank account details.

Security and locking rules

  • Company data is isolated: users only see their own company unless they are SUPER_ADMIN.
  • Role permissions control view/create/edit/delete/print for each module; the database re-checks every mutation.
  • Customer logins are linked to specific customers and can only create sales orders for those customers.
  • Documents older than 3 days, or dated on/before the entry lock date, are locked. Super Admin, Company Admin or a bypass user can override.
  • A document with a downstream record is immutable: SO with GDN, GDN with invoice, invoice with receipt.
  • Sessions expire after 12 hours; company licence/demo locks can block login entirely.
